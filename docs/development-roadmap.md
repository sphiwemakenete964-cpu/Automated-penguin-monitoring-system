\# PCB Development Roadmap



This document outlines the main stages involved in my PCB development

work for the Automated Penguin Monitoring System (APMS).



\## Stage 1: Understanding the Supplied Circuit



\- Reviewed the circuit schematic supplied by the supervisor/project team.

\- Identified the components and their electrical connections.

\- Retained the original circuit design as instructed.



\## Stage 2: Component Footprint Assignment



\- Worked on assigning suitable PCB footprints.

\- Considered the physical requirements of the components.



\## Stage 3: PCB Layout



\- Transferred the circuit into the PCB layout environment.

\- Arranged components on the board.

\- Considered component spacing and board dimensions.



\## Stage 4: Routing and Copper Zones



\- Routed PCB tracks.

\- Configured copper zones and pours.

\- Investigated connectivity issues.



\## Stage 5: Design Verification



\- Investigated design-rule violations.

\- Worked through clearance issues.

\- Examined thermal relief and zone connectivity.

\- Considered mechanical design constraints.



\## Stage 6: Documentation



\- Documented the PCB development process.

\- Recorded engineering challenges and learning outcomes.

\- Organised the project documentation using Git and GitHub.



\## Development Scope



This roadmap describes the PCB implementation activities.

It does not imply that the original circuit was designed or modified

by me, or that final fabrication and testing were completed.
