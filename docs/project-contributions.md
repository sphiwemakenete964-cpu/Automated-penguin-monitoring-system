\# My Contribution to the APMS Project



\## Role and Responsibilities



My contribution to the Automated Penguin Monitoring System (APMS) focused on the printed circuit board (PCB) implementation.



The circuit schematic was supplied by the supervisor/project team. I was instructed to retain the original circuit design without modifying its electrical connections or component values.



My responsibility was to translate the supplied circuit into a physical PCB layout using KiCad 10.



\## Work Undertaken



My PCB development activities included:



\- Assigning suitable footprints to electronic components.

\- Arranging components on the PCB.

\- Routing electrical connections between components.

\- Configuring copper zones and pours.

\- Considering PCB dimensions and mounting-hole placement.

\- Investigating design-rule and clearance violations.

\- Troubleshooting copper-zone connectivity and thermal relief issues.

\- Considering PCB manufacturing constraints.



\## Engineering Skills Developed



Through this work, I gained practical experience in:



\- Electronic PCB design and layout.

\- Component footprint selection.

\- PCB routing and connectivity.

\- KiCad design-rule checking.

\- Hardware design considerations.

\- Technical troubleshooting and engineering problem-solving.



\## Tools Used



\- \*\*KiCad 10:\*\* PCB implementation and layout.

\- \*\*Git:\*\* Version control.

\- \*\*GitHub:\*\* Project documentation and portfolio management.



\## Reflection



This project provided practical exposure to the process of translating an existing electronic circuit into a physical PCB layout.



It strengthened my understanding of PCB development, design constraints, component placement and the importance of maintaining electrical and mechanical considerations throughout the design process.
